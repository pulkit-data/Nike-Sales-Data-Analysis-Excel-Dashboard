# Insights
- Kolkata generated the highest revenue among all cities (₹1.65 Lakhs).
- Online channel slightly outperformed Retail, generating ₹4 Lakhs compared to ₹3.86 Lakhs from Retail.
- Flex Trainer is the top-performing product by revenue.
- Men contribute the highest overall revenue (approximately ₹3.1 Lakhs), significantly more than Women and Kids. 


# Recomendations
- Focus digital marketing efforts on Women’s Lifestyle products in the Online channel. Although this category currently has limited Online sales, it has shown strong performance in Retail, indicating untapped potential in the digital space.
